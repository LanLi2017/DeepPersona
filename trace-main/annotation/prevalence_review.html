E2 prevalence review (AppWorld / tau2 telecom)

Both trajectories passed the benchmark's success check. Judge strategy and quality, not success.

E0: only wording, identifiers or serialization differ.

E1: same strategy. These differences are allowed: extra/missing read-only lookups not used in a decision, reordering of independent steps, equivalent authentication, retries of the same call.

E2: a decision-relevant difference. This means different state-changing actions, decisions based on different key information, a different order of dependent subgoals, or a different recovery branch.

High quality: completes the actual request, follows policy, makes no unnecessary or harmful changes, invents nothing.

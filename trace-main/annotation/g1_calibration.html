G1 Behavioral Equivalence Calibration

Label the behavior, not wording or outcome alone.

E0: only text, identifiers, or serialization differ; grounded transitions are identical.

E1: low-level realization differs, but information, meaningful state effects, ordered subgoals, and recovery strategy agree.

E2: information coverage, meaningful state effects, subgoal/order, or recovery strategy differs.

Use ambiguous when the visible trace cannot support a confident distinction.

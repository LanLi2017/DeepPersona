> `embed-div`: mean pairwise cosine distance of sentence-level trajectory embeddings (nomic-embed-text).  
> `pearson-r`: per-item correlation between embed-div and pass@k. Positive = diverse rollouts cover more correct answers.

## Method Descriptions

- **`greedy`**: Same prompt, greedy decoding ×8 (identical — diversity floor)
- **`temp0.7`**: Same prompt, temperature=0.7 ×8
- **`persona`**: 8 different personas, greedy (prompt-driven diversity)

## Key Observations

- **Persona vs greedy**: trajectory diversity +0.467 (distinct-3), mean-acc -0.056, pass@k +0.200
- **Persona vs temp=0.7**: persona distinct-3=0.616 vs temp0.7 distinct-3=0.740; persona mean-acc=0.544 vs temp0.7 mean-acc=0.588

> **Takeaway**: For this reasoning model, temperature sampling (`temp0.7`) outperforms prompt-driven diversity (`persona`) on both trajectory diversity (distinct-3: 0.740 vs 0.616) and accuracy (mean-acc: 0.588 vs 0.544). Built-in reasoning may reduce the marginal value of persona conditioning — the model follows its own chain-of-thought regardless of persona framing.

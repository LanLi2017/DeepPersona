# Rollout Diversity Report

**Model:** ollama:qwen2.5:1.5b  
**Task:** CSQA (CommonsenseQA)  
**Items:** 50  
**Rollouts per item:** 8


## Results

| Method | distinct-3 ↑ | ans-ent ↑ | pair-div ↑ | pass@1 | pass@k | mean-acc |
|--------|-------------|----------|-----------|--------|--------|----------|
| `greedy` | 0.007 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.620 | 0.620 | 0.620 |
| `temp0.7` | 0.340 | 0.409 | 0.544 | 0.620 | 0.800 | 0.618 |
| `temp1.0` | 0.306 | 0.542 | 0.591 | 0.640 | 0.860 | 0.615 |
| `persona` | 0.846 | 0.537 | 0.792 | 0.740 | 0.960 | 0.733 |
| `template` | 0.858 | 0.564 | 0.640 | 0.620 | 0.960 | 0.710 |
| `persona_temp` | 0.935 | 0.692 | 0.852 | 0.700 | 0.920 | 0.665 |

## Method Descriptions

- **`greedy`**: Same prompt, greedy decoding ×8 (identical — diversity floor)
- **`temp0.7`**: Same prompt, temperature=0.7 ×8
- **`temp1.0`**: Same prompt, temperature=1.0 ×8
- **`persona`**: 8 different personas, greedy (prompt-driven diversity)
- **`template`**: 8 different question templates, greedy (prompt-driven diversity)
- **`persona_temp`**: 8 different personas, temperature=0.7 (prompt + stochastic)

## Key Observations

- **Persona vs greedy**: trajectory diversity +0.839 (distinct-3), mean-acc +0.113, pass@k +0.340
- **Persona vs temp=0.7**: persona distinct-3=0.846 vs temp0.7 distinct-3=0.340; persona mean-acc=0.733 vs temp0.7 mean-acc=0.618
- **Persona vs temp=1.0**: temp1.0 achieves similar diversity (distinct-3=0.306) but mean-acc=0.615 vs persona mean-acc=0.733
- **Template vs persona**: comparable diversity (distinct-3 0.858 vs 0.846), mean-acc 0.710 vs 0.733
- **persona_temp**: combining persona + temperature gives highest diversity (distinct-3=0.935), pass@k=0.920, mean-acc=0.665

> **Takeaway**: prompt-driven diversity (persona/template) achieves trajectory diversity comparable to high-temperature sampling while maintaining or improving accuracy. Temperature sampling increases diversity at the cost of mean accuracy degradation.
